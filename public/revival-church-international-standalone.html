The RCI Discipleship Pathway

Sunday morning is only the beginning. The Pathway is our structured 6-step journey designed to take you from a first-time guest to an empowered, purpose-driven kingdom leader.

1 Week 1

Step 01: Connect & Welcome

Stop by the VIP Welcome Lounge after any Sunday service. Enjoy great coffee, meet friendly hosts, and receive your Welcome Gift Pack.

2 Week 2

Step 02: Meet RCI & Senior Leaders

An informal dessert reception with our campus pastors. Learn our history, core apostolic values, and divine mission.

3 Week 3

Step 03: Foundations 101

A 2-session module uncovering new-creation realities, righteousness by faith, water baptism, and authority in Christ.

4 Week 4

Step 04: Join the V-Team (Serving)

Discover your spiritual gifts and passions. Shadow active serving teams across worship, media, kids, and hospitality.

5 Week 5

Step 05: Empowerment 201

Deep activation into hearing God's voice, praying in the Spirit, and practical demonstrations of divine love and power.

6 Week 6

Step 06: Full Commissioning

Celebration of completion! Formally received into covenant church membership, ready to lead an eGroup or marketplace altar.
